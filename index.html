Umang Garg

Ph.D. Researcher @ UC Santa Barbara | Physics-Informed Generative AI | Inventor & Builder

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Engineering at UC Santa Barbara, working in the Vision Research Lab with Prof. B. S. Manjunath. My research develops generative and vision models that encode physical structure, with current work in symmetry-group-aware EBSD super-resolution and conditional diffusion for 3D/4D material microstructures.

My broader work spans trajectory foundation models, medical image segmentation, adversarial robustness, and neuromorphic computing. I also build applied AI systems for education and surgical planning, connecting rigorous research to problems with direct human value.

I am the lead inventor on two U.S. patent applications, a first author on research across scientific AI, neuromorphic hardware, and communication systems, and a recipient of two UCSB fellowships.
